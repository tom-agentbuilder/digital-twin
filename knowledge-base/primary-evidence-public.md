## E36 — Competitor Intelligence Through Talent Access

**Tags:** competitor-intelligence, global-marketing, business-intelligence, expert-consultation

A Global Marketing team wanted to understand the market positioning and dynamics of a key competitor and identified a senior individual with relevant industry experience. Tom was asked to support outreach and arrange a paid expert consultation.

His role was to identify the right person, establish the relationship, structure the conversation and keep the discussion focused on commercially relevant, non-confidential industry insights.

The lesson Tom took from this was that TA can create value beyond candidate access. Direct access to the talent market can become a source of business intelligence when the recruiter can identify the right person, build trust, ask useful questions and translate the conversation into something useful for the business.

This reinforces Tom's view that strong TA can sit closer to business strategy than traditional transactional recruiting suggests.

**Source:** Interview Story — Competitor Intelligence.

**Use for:** business acumen, stakeholder partnership, market intelligence, relationship building and TA-as-business-function questions.
